# English

## Privacy Policy for SMExpress

BHC Tecnologia built **SMExpress** as a free-to-use mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information SMExpress and the third-party services used by the app may collect, how that information is used, and where your data is stored.

### Information Stored on Your Device

SMExpress can be used without an account ("guest mode"). In guest mode, all data is stored locally on your device, including:

* Households, shopping lists and their items
* App settings and preferences
* A locally generated guest identifier

Guest data is not synchronized to the cloud. If you later create an account or sign in, your local households are copied to your account so they can be synchronized and shared.

### Firebase Authentication

SMExpress uses **Firebase Authentication** to provide user authentication.

Depending on the sign-in method used, Firebase may process information associated with your authentication account, such as:

* Firebase user ID (UID)
* Email address
* Display name
* Authentication provider information (for example, Google Sign-In)

Passwords are handled entirely by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password.

### Cloud Data

When you are signed in, SMExpress synchronizes data with **Firebase Firestore** so that it can be shared between members of a household and across your devices. This may include:

* Household names and membership
* Shopping lists and their items (names, quantities, notes, checked state)
* A per-household catalog of previously bought items, used to offer item suggestions while typing

### Analytics

SMExpress uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** to collect information about how the application is used.

Analytics may include information such as:

* App usage and interaction events (for example, a list being created or an item being checked off)
* Screen views
* Device and app information
* Session and diagnostic information

Analytics events contain counts and flags only — never the content of your lists, items, or household names.

### Crash Reporting

SMExpress uses **Firebase Crashlytics** to help identify and fix application crashes and other technical problems.

Crash reports may contain information such as:

* Device and operating system information
* Application version
* Crash information and stack traces
* Application state and diagnostic information
* A user identifier (your account ID, or the local guest ID when not signed in) used to estimate how many users are affected by an issue

Crash data is processed by Google Firebase for the purpose of diagnosing and improving application stability.

### Camera

SMExpress uses the device camera solely to scan QR codes when adding a member to a household. No images or camera data are stored or transmitted.

### Advertising

SMExpress does not display advertisements and does not use advertising networks or advertising identifiers.

### Data Sharing

BHC Tecnologia does not sell users' personal information.

SMExpress uses third-party service providers to provide authentication, cloud synchronization, analytics, and crash reporting services. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their respective services.

The principal third-party services used by SMExpress are:

* Google Firebase Authentication
* Google Firebase Firestore
* Google Analytics for Firebase
* Firebase Crashlytics

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

### Data Security

SMExpress uses appropriate technical measures to protect information transmitted between the application and its cloud services. All network traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS).

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and BHC Tecnologia cannot guarantee absolute security.

### Data Retention and Deletion

Local data remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user or removed as part of normal application or device management.

Cloud-synchronized data may remain in Firebase services while associated with the user's account and while necessary to provide the application's synchronization features.

You can delete your account and associated cloud data directly in the app: **Settings → Delete account**.

If you would like to request information about or deletion of data associated with your SMExpress account, please contact us using the email address below.

### Children's Privacy

SMExpress is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, the services we use, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be made available at the location where this Privacy Policy is published.

### Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or SMExpress's handling of information, please contact:

**BHC Tecnologia**
**Email:** [support@bhcsoftware.com.br](mailto:support@bhcsoftware.com.br)

O SMExpress usa o **Firebase Crashlytics** para ajudar a identificar e corrigir falhas e outros problemas técnicos do aplicativo.

Os relatórios de falha podem conter informações como:

* Informações do dispositivo e do sistema operacional
* Versão do aplicativo

Os dados de falhas são processados pelo Google Firebase com a finalidade de diagnosticar e melhorar a estabilidade do aplicativo.

O SMExpress usa a câmera do dispositivo exclusivamente para ler códigos QR ao adicionar um membro ao domicílio. Nenhuma imagem ou dado da câmera é armazenado ou transmitido.

A BHC Tecnologia não vende informações pessoais de usuários.

* Les foyers, les listes de courses et leurs articles

* Un identifiant d'invité généré localement

SMExpress utilise **Firebase Authentication** pour l'authentification des utilisateurs.

Selon la méthode de connexion utilisée, Firebase peut traiter des informations associées à votre compte d'authentification, telles que :

* L'identifiant utilisateur Firebase (UID)
* L'adresse e-mail
* Le nom d'affichage
* Les informations du fournisseur d'authentification (par exemple, Google Sign-In)

Lorsque vous êtes connecté, SMExpress synchronise les données avec **Firebase Firestore** afin qu'elles puissent être partagées entre les membres d'un foyer et entre vos appareils. Cela peut inclure :

### Rapports de plantage

SMExpress utilise **Firebase Crashlytics** pour identifier et corriger les plantages et autres problèmes techniques de l'application.

Les rapports de plantage peuvent contenir des informations telles que :

* Les informations sur l'appareil et le système d'exploitation
* La version de l'application
* Les informations sur le plantage et les traces d'appels
* L'état de l'application et les informations de diagnostic
