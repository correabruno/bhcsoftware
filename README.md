# English

## Privacy Policy for BHC Sudoku

BHC Tecnologia built **BHC Sudoku** as a free-to-use mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information BHC Sudoku and the third-party services used by the app may collect, how that information is used, and where your game data is stored.

### Information Stored on Your Device

BHC Sudoku stores certain game data locally on your device, including:

* Game settings and preferences
* Current puzzle and game progress
* Game history
* Game-related profile information, such as tip tokens and other game-issued data

Your current game progress is primarily stored locally and is not continuously uploaded to the cloud as you make moves.

### Firebase Authentication

BHC Sudoku uses **Firebase Authentication** to provide user authentication.

Depending on the sign-in method used, Firebase may process information associated with your authentication account, such as:

* Firebase user ID (UID)
* Email address
* Display name
* Authentication provider information

Anonymous users may use the application without creating a permanent account.

Authentication information is processed by Google Firebase according to Google's privacy practices.

### Cloud Game Data

When you are signed in with a non-anonymous account, BHC Sudoku may synchronize certain game-related data with **Firebase Firestore** so that your data can be available across supported installations.

This may include:

* Game settings
* Game history
* Game-related profile information, including tip-token information and other game-issued properties

The current active game is designed to remain on the device rather than being continuously synchronized with Firestore.

Anonymous users do not have their game data synchronized to Firestore.

### Analytics

BHC Sudoku uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** to collect information about how the application is used.

Analytics may include information such as:

* App usage and interaction events
* Device and app information
* Session and diagnostic information
* General information used to understand application usage and improve the app

Analytics data is processed by Google according to Google's privacy policies.

### Crash Reporting

BHC Sudoku uses **Firebase Crashlytics** to help identify and fix application crashes and other technical problems.

Crash reports may contain information such as:

* Device and operating system information
* Application version
* Crash information and stack traces
* Application state and diagnostic information

Crashlytics may automatically collect crash reports depending on the application's configuration.

Crash data is processed by Google Firebase for the purpose of diagnosing and improving application stability.

### Advertising

BHC Sudoku uses **Google AdMob** to provide optional rewarded advertisements.

Users may voluntarily watch rewarded advertisements to receive in-game rewards.

AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interaction information, and other information used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

Depending on the user's location, applicable privacy choices, and Google's advertising configuration, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google's privacy and consent mechanisms may provide users with choices regarding personalized advertising and the processing of advertising-related data.

### Data Sharing

BHC Tecnologia does not sell users' personal information.

BHC Sudoku uses third-party service providers to provide authentication, cloud synchronization, analytics, crash reporting, and advertising services. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their respective services.

The principal third-party services used by BHC Sudoku are:

* Google Firebase Authentication
* Google Firebase Firestore
* Google Analytics for Firebase
* Firebase Crashlytics
* Google AdMob

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

### Data Security

BHC Sudoku uses appropriate technical measures to protect information transmitted between the application and its cloud services.

Game data that does not need to be synchronized with the cloud remains stored locally on the user's device.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and BHC Tecnologia cannot guarantee absolute security.

### Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user or removed as part of normal application or device management.

Cloud-synchronized game data may remain in Firebase services while associated with the user's account and while necessary to provide the application's synchronization features.

If you would like to request information about or deletion of data associated with your BHC Sudoku account, please contact us using the email address below.

### Children's Privacy

BHC Sudoku is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, the services we use, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be made available at the location where this Privacy Policy is published.

### Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or BHC Sudoku's handling of information, please contact:

**BHC Tecnologia**
**Email:** [support@bhcsoftware.com.br](mailto:support@bhcsoftware.com.br)
